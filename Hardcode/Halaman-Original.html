About the Dynamic M75B

The Dynamic M75B Active Noise-Cancelling Wireless Headphones deliver detailed, serious acoustics and custom-tailored sound with 40mm Beryllium drivers and Adaptive Active Noise Cancellation for the perfect combination of sound and silence.

Experience effortless all-day listening with lightweight anodized aluminum and soft lambskin leather in a distinct, refined silhouette — built for professionals who demand both performance and elegance.

Featuring TUMI's iconic design language, the M75B is a seamless blend of engineering precision and artistic expression, designed to enhance every journey whether in the office, the cabin, or the city.

Key Features

Adaptive ANC

Three ANC modes — Adaptive, MAX, and All Day — let you tune out the world on your terms.

40mm Beryllium Drivers

Proprietary beryllium-coated drivers deliver expansive, detailed sound with exceptional clarity.

32-Hour Battery

Up to 32 hours of playtime. Up to 28 hours with ANC enabled, so your music lasts as long as you do.

Bluetooth 5.1

Stable 30m wireless range with AAC, aptX™ Adaptive up to 24-bit/48kHz, and SBC codec support.

Premium Materials

Lambskin leather headband and ear pads with anodized aluminum controls for lasting comfort.

4 Microphone Array

Four-microphone talk and ANC solution with wind protection for crystal-clear calls anywhere.
